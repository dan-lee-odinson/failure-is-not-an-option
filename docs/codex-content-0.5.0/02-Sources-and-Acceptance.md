## Primary-source reading added for cadence

- **H6: Gemini VIII mission report**, NASA MSC-G-R-66-4, 29 April 1966. Read printed pp. 6-8–6-11 (PDF 376–379) and visually inspected 6-9. [Archival mirror of the NASA report](https://apollojournals.org/alsj/43455667-Gemini-Program-Mission-Report-Gemini-Viii.pdf). The original NASA-hosted path returned 404 during this review. Page 6-9 records zone 3 availability on revolutions 6 and 7, with revolution 7 chosen. That is a historical timing choice; it does not verify the menu’s particular recovery-force, propellant-warning, or crew-exhaustion model. Page 6-8 also distinguishes real-time RCS estimates from later calculations, so no precise fuel quantity is invented in the player’s display.
- **H7: voice transcription**, [NASA-hosted Gemini VIII air-ground/onboard transcript](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/gt08-tec.pdf), PDF pp. 75–79; PDF 77 visually checked. Short calls, repeat requests, readbacks, uncertain information, and onboard crew action informed original dialogue. This is not a complete Houston flight-loop transcript. No game dialogue is presented as verbatim, and noisy OCR readings were not converted into numeric game telemetry.
- **H8: Gene Kranz memoir**, [official publisher’s Chapter One excerpt](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Failure-Is-Not-an-Option/Gene-Kranz/9781439148815). The Mercury training and communications passages informed rehearsal vocabulary. This publicly available excerpt is not the Gemini VIII chapter. H5 remains the secondary basis for the post-flight positions; the original memoir passages behind that article have not been independently checked.

The mission report’s historical timing choice strengthens the basis for an interesting operational fork. It does not require relabeling either authored route as the exact historical recovery.
