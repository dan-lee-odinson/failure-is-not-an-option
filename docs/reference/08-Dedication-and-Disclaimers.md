# Failure is Not an Option — Dedication and Disclaimers

**Status:** Proposed player-facing wording for the user's review. These statements reflect the intended project and development process; confirm they remain accurate for the version ultimately distributed.

## Dedication

To the men and women of NASA, whose bravery, sacrifice, and vision embody the best of humanity.

To those who journeyed beyond our world, and to all whose work made those journeys possible.

## Project disclaimer

Failure is Not an Option is an independent homage to NASA and the people of the space program, created for appreciation and exploration, not for profit. It is not affiliated with, authorized, sponsored, or endorsed by NASA.

## Generative AI disclosure

This project was created using generative AI within a human-in-the-loop process, with human creative direction, review, and final decision-making.

## Historical dramatization

Inspired by real missions and people, this game includes fictional dialogue, simplified systems, and alternate historical outcomes. These elements do not represent the actual words, beliefs, or actions of the people portrayed. NASA is not responsible for the game's interpretations, generated content, or accuracy.

## Presentation proposal

- Show the dedication on its own restrained card before the historical montage. Give the words space; do not combine them with the disclaimer text. A brief silent prelude can preserve the full soundtrack timeline.
- Present the project disclaimer and AI disclosure on a readable information screen before the first opening, with a Continue control and no forced countdown. Keep all notices permanently accessible through About/Credits.
- Put the historical-dramatization statement on that information screen or the New Campaign screen, and retain it in About/Credits and the repository README.
- Use application-rendered text, readable sizing, keyboard access, and player-controlled reading time. The dedication may be skipped with the opening. These notices are acknowledgments, not a license agreement requiring the player to accept terms.

## Production note: attribution and NASA imagery

The user has chosen an original fictional emblem for new game art while preserving marks already present in original archival footage. See [09-Original-Emblem-and-Archive-Policy.md](09-Original-Emblem-and-Archive-Policy.md) for that distinction and the first Flight Operations concept.

Keep original archival material and generated illustrations identifiable in the credits and asset manifest. Attribute the archival sources and describe generated contributions as part of this independent project; do not suggest NASA reviewed or approved them.

NASA's current media guidance includes an AI section stating that its insignia, worm logotype, and seal should not appear with AI-generated imagery. This is an art-production constraint to account for separately from the disclaimer, especially when planning generated control-room images, uniforms, and patches. The notices do not themselves grant rights to use footage, logos, music, or third-party material.

Source checked for this draft: [NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/), including the source-disclosure, endorsement, and AI-application provisions.
