# Failure is Not an Option — Added Post-flight Accountability Branch

**Package:** 0.4.0 · **Direction:** Dan's AmericaSpace excerpt and explicit additional decision. **Status:** Authored content ready for integration; no runtime-test claim.

The new branch supplements the operational return decision in 0.3.0. It does not replace that decision or reinstate the discarded report-filing mechanic.

| Player stance | Fictional consequence |
|---|---|
| Agree with Cunningham and Stafford's reported criticism; blame Armstrong and Scott | Armstrong and Scott each lose 2 trust toward Glen. Cunningham and Stafford each gain 1, representing support from the critics. The astronaut corps gains a persistent division condition. |
| Follow Kranz's reported focus on missing ground contingencies | Armstrong and Scott each gain 1 trust; the critics' trust stays unchanged. Glen accepts ground responsibility and commissions docked-flight contingency development. The existing disagreement remains in the historical context. |

The [AmericaSpace article](https://www.americaspace.com/2014/03/16/tumbling-end-over-end-the-trauma-of-gemini-viii-part-2/) is recorded as H5. Its criticism is preserved with attribution alongside its statement that the crew were cleared. The historical option follows the reported Kranz position. Glen's words, the compressed scene, trust values, deepened rift and specific commissioned task are labeled F10 fiction, not exact historical events or direct quotations.

The player can blame the crew without the game changing its account of the crew's actions to make that blame true. The social consequences express how Glen's leadership changes this campaign. They persist into the Gemini IX-A briefing as text-only relationship states and a corps-division or contingency-work status. No new portraits are needed.

The existing six return outcomes, Mara/Elias relationships and operational IX-A plan restrictions remain intact. The added choice can combine with every return outcome and either baseline lesson. AC-15 and AC-16 cover the added divergence, evidence timing, response boundaries, save/replay and exactly-once trust/task effects; all sixteen acceptance cases remain for Claude to execute in the build.
