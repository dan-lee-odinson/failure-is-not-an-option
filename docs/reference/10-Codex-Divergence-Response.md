## The decision

Glen signs either a complete initial flight account or a milestone summary that explicitly defers the required full account. The player sees both drafts and the fictional completeness standard before choosing. A separate visible PROGRAM REVIEW event then accepts or returns the account and releases or reserves the director's discretionary slot in the next planning session.

| Choice | Persisted consequence | Gemini IX-A availability |
|---|---|---|
| Sign the complete initial account | Account accepted; discretionary planning slot released | Director's supplemental-agenda reference card is readable. |
| Send milestone summary and defer the complete account | Account returned as incomplete; discretionary slot reserved for correction | Supplemental-agenda card is disabled; required-account-correction card is readable. |

Both branches end in safe recovery. Deferral is the avoidable error; it carries unfinished accountability into the next session. The milestone summary is not a fabricated report of total mission success. Neither choice asserts that the engineering investigation is complete.

The administrative process, review function, standard, and slot consequences are explicitly labeled original dramatization F7. They are not attributed to a real official, agency policy, or dispute about Armstrong and Scott. No new historical assertion needs to be smuggled into the existing H1–H4 source register.

## Contract fit and practical limits

- **SC-07:** reading the standard reveals the policy; it never grants physical capability. The signed submission and subsequent review event change administrative facts only.
- **SC-08:** each causal debrief rule requires the submission, completed review, disposition, and slot effect. The log records the draft and the separate review effects. Submission alone cannot support an acceptance claim.
- **Real carryover:** separate facts persist and separate reference IDs are actually available. The held route cannot open the supplemental card or clear the hold by browsing the correction card.
- **Bounded implementation:** linear phases and one deterministic conditional review event; no chance, numerical political-capital system, new attention economy, or additional portraits. There is an explicit saveable receipt before review.
- **Verification requested:** AC-13 compares paired runs differing only at the new decision; AC-14 covers review boundaries, save/import/replay, invalid branch combinations, and content-version mismatch. AC-01 through AC-12 now default to the complete-account route.

This first consequence is deliberately clear, not a difficult strategic balancing problem. M00 ends at the next briefing, so the slot loss is enforced through reference availability; it does not yet simulate a lost training exercise or its later mission effects. Larger political and operational tradeoffs remain campaign work.
